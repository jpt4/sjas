A third possible leg of a proposed Tripod reply to Hilbert’s second problem involves formal

systems using Fixed Point axiomatic sentences that confirm their own consistency. For example,

[38, 39, 41, 42, 47] examined systems strictly weaker than PA, that verified their own self-

consistency, under mostly semantic tableau deduction [13, 32].
