The method for encoding a proof will be similar to a formula’s
encoding, except that it will need a 56-th symbol (called the
period symbol) for separating the distinct formulae that appear
in sequential order in the proof. As before, the sequence of n
digits D,, Do, D3, ... Dn , describing a proof, will again
be written as a number in base 64, whose value falls between
