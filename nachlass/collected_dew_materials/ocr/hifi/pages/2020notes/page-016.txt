And so the results about PA+ turn out to be easier to prove than their analogs

about PA, although the latter is only a conservative extension of the former.

On the other hand, there is a historic reason why logicians have chosen mostly
to work in the framework of PA, rather than PA+. It is that PA is the historic
language of the simplest branch of Mathematics, since its basic function primitives
are Addition and Multiplication (and nothing more). Gédel’s 1931 version of the
Incompleteness Theorem shocked the world of Mathematics because it showed that

undecidability results arose in such a foundational branch of Mathematics.

It also answered an open question that was explicitly raised by the mathematician

David Hilbert.
