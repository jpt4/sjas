Quick Notes on the Incompleteness Theorem (Oct. 4, 2020)

Dan E. Willard *

The October 2020 Expansion of the April 2010 draft (that was broader than the
initial March 2004 draft by including Léb’s Theorem)

Goal of these Notes: These lecture notes are intended to summarize the
intuition behind Gédel’s First and Second Incompleteness Theorem. I believe that
they should be easier to comprehend than Mendelson’s treatment [4] of the subject.
They assume the reader is familiar with Sections 1.1 through 1.4, Sections 2.1 through
2.6 and Section 3.1 of his textbook.

These notes are not intended as a replacement for the Sections 3.2 through 3.5 of
Mendelson’s textbook. Rather they are intended to prove a slightly weaker theorem
that captures the main intuition behind those sections in a small amount of page

space.

The current 2020 draft is an amended version of prior notes I used during 2004-
2018.
