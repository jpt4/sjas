## 3. Distilled theses

1. **The chasm** between Hilbert's Consistency Program and Second
   Incompleteness was immediate in 1931; neither Hilbert nor early Gödel
   dismissed compromise solutions (§1).
2. **Sacks recollection** (footnote): Gödel told Gerald Sacks Hilbert's
   program was "very much alive and even more interesting" — later expanded
   in `Willard2020-LFCS` / obligation **O77**.
3. **Why study exceptions**: ecological survival (Global Warming named) needs
   formalisms that know their own consistency and the `Π₁` implications of
   PA; short proofs of universe-scale integers are not required (Abstract, §1).
4. **Type-NS / S / A / M** taxonomy restated (Remark 2.5); Theorem `++`
   (Pudlák–Solovay): no natural Type-S system recognizes its own Hilbert
   consistency.
5. **Enriched tableaux** (Definition 3.11): Rank-k, Rank-Zero, Rank-Zero-Plus
   enrichments via Law-of-Excluded-Middle variants; Linear Constrained Cut
   Rule (Def 3.8). Infinite enrichments make tableaux resemble Hilbert
   (Lemma 3.10) — the engineering intuition behind later Xtab.
6. **Boundary claim (Remark 3.12)**: `IS_D(α)` works for conventional and
   Rank-Zero / Rank-Zero-Plus enriched tableaux; Type-S systems fail under
   **infinite** enrichments (the remark restricts the Type-S failure to that
   case, not to enrichment in general); Rank-2+ under Type-A reactivates
   Second Incompleteness; Rank-1 left as an open question in this draft.
7. **Three "almost cheating" style objections** to `IS_D(α)` answered in §5–6:
   Type-A weakness, weak tableaux apparatus, reliance on an "I am consistent"
   axiom — the same objection `Willard2016` (Dec 2016) had already put as its
   statement `###` / Platonic Stability, restated here two years later. The
   phrase "almost cheating" is `Willard2016`'s, not this paper's.
8. **Authorial caveat**: treat as preliminary relative to `Willard2020`.
