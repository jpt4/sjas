## 2. Role in corpus

Unpublished March 2008 set-theory program claiming ZF/ZFC can prove their own
inconsistency (non-constructive Theorems 2–3; constructive Corollary 2), via
Boolean-4 truth values, SupportSet / Paradox-ZF(C), Choose*, and a proposed
repair **WZF** (bounded-set-quantifier Replacement). Notarized drafts
(Mar 8 / 14 / 18 / 21). **Outside** the published SJAS arithmetic line; the
synthesis's "possibly ZF manuscript". Finite-set SJAS reading remains O36 /
`Willard2001`, not these drafts.

## 3. Per-file map (image-verified)

### `ZFnote.pdf` (1 pp., notarized Mar 8, 2008 — James Hershey, Saratoga Co.)

Title: March 8 Notes for "On the Formally Proven Inconsistency of Zermello
Frankel Set Theory…". Narrative of Mar 1–8 work. Notation items 1–15 (INT,
P₁, P₂, ENUM_L, Decipher, SupportSet, Choose, ZFC, Support-ZFC/ZF). Theorem 1
= Carnap–Gödel Fixed Point. Introduces Paradox-ZFC / Paradox-ZF.

### `20080314_ZCF_notes.pdf` (6 pp., notarized Mar 14 — Tina Herbst / Albany)

Title: March 14 Notes for "On Some Difficulties…". Theorem 2 (ZFC proves own
inconsistency, non-constructive); Theorem 3 (ZF likewise); Corollary 2
(constructive ZF inconsistency). Notation: Boolean-4 (T/F/B/U), Decipher1–4,
SupportSet, Choose*, Top-Good, Paradox-ZFC/ZF. Statement ++: ∀k ∃n>k ZF_n ⊢
Cons(ZF_k). Appendix sketch via Gentzen / Takeuti. Proposes **WZF**. Refs 1–23.

### `20080318_ZCF_notes.pdf` (8 pp., notarized Mar 18 — David J. Slater, Jr.)

Polished variant of the March 14 line; same Theorem 2/3 / Corollary 2 /
Boolean-4 / Paradox / WZF apparatus. Handwritten "BACK SIDE noturized" on
references page. Refs ~22.

### `20080321_ZCF_notes_notepage7doubled.pdf` (9 pp., notarized Mar 21 — Scott R. Bechard, Saratoga)

"More yet polished version"; alternate titles listed. Full Notation through
items 21–22 (Top-Good, Choose*). Lemma 3 (+), Paradox-ZF (***), Theorem 3,
Corollaries 1–2, Statement ++, WZF section ("Old Section 5 to be removed"),
Refs 1–22, Footnote 4 (finite-subset consistency ≠ infinite-union).

### `DOC102924-10292024193202.pdf` (2 pp.)

(Low-ZF discussion; Statement ++; Empty / InconsCF / InconsHilb). Not a
