## 2. Role in corpus

Late-period **teaching** presentation of Gödel I/II and Löb, aimed at
students who know Mendelson §§1.1–1.4, 2.1–2.6, 3.1. Captures Willard's
classroom vocabulary (PA+, Cons(α), Regular Δ₀ systems, U-Grounding) and
his own pointer to `Willard2001` as the longest JSL 2001 paper on logics that
evade Second Incompleteness (PDF p. 81 / printed ~42). Not a source of new
SJAS theorems.
