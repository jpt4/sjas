## 3. Distilled theses

1. **Boundary-case exception** is the deliberate phrase: full-fledged
   exceptions cannot exist; the Second Incompleteness Theorem is too strong
   (p. 2).
2. Two scholarly motives: (i) classify maximum generalizations and partial
   limitations; (ii) partially formalize humans' instinctive faith that
   thinking is useful (pp. 2–3).
3. **Drop multiplication-totality**; treat `M(x,y,z)` as a 3-way predicate;
   still prove all PA's `Π₁⁻` theorems (p. 3).
4. **Kleene's `Diagonal(α,D)` / `α^D`**: well-defined but typically
   inconsistent; the research finds when `α` is weak enough that `α^D`
   escapes (pp. 3–4).
5. **Pudlák–Solovay barrier**: no consistent system can simultaneously
   recognize successor as total, treat Add/Mult as non-total relations, and
   recognize its own Hilbert consistency (p. 4).
